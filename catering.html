Good to know

Ordering & delivery

🚚

Free delivery over $350

Free within an 8 km radius of either venue on orders over $350. Orders under $350 incur a $50 flat delivery charge. Beyond 8 km, delivery is charged at $2 per km.

📅

48 hours' notice

Please order at least 48 hours ahead so our kitchen can prepare everything fresh. Cold Selections are pre-order only.

🛍

Free pickup, 11:30am–8pm

Prefer to collect? Pick up free of charge between 11:30am and 8:00pm from The Fairway or The Jack & Cleaver. Outside these hours may incur a charge.

🥗

Dietary needs

We cater for dietary needs — just ask. Look for V vegetarian, VG vegan and GF gluten free on the menu. All prices in AUD, GST inclusive.

Prefer to talk it through?

We're here to help

Catering a bigger event, or not sure what you need? Our team is happy to talk through quantities, dietaries and timing before you order.
