Two venues, every occasion catered for

Whether you're planning an intimate gathering or a celebration for three hundred, our function packages are designed to suit whatever the occasion — from relaxed grazing tables and canapés, to generous buffets, shared banquets, or a plated set menu.

Our packages are available at both of our venues, so you can choose the setting that suits your event best.

The Fairway — Penrith Golf Club, with unobstructed 180° views of the greens. Main Terrace and Small Terrace available.
The Jack & Cleaver — Pennant Hills Bowling Club, a warm and versatile space overlooking the green. Yarrara Function Room and Mezzanine available.
